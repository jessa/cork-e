Privacy policy

Cork-e Klok and Cork-e Kalender · last updated 18 October 2026

In short

  • We only store what the clock and the calendar need to work.
  • Everything is on our own server in Germany.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We don't sell or share anything.
  • Your events stay in Google Calendar: we keep no copy of them.
  • One email to info@cork-e.eu is enough to have everything erased.

Who is responsible?

Cork-e is a project by Jessa Bekker, Belgium. Cork-e is still in development and is being tested with a small group of families. For any privacy question: info@cork-e.eu.

Which data, and what for?

Your account

You sign in with your Google account. Google then gives us your name, your email address and a fixed identifier of your Google account. We use these to recognise you. We get no access to your password or your email.

After you sign in, we set one cookie that keeps you signed in (at most 180 days). While you sign in or connect Google Calendar, we also use a 10-minute cookie to check that Google's answer belongs to your request. We use no other cookies.

Your family

  • Your family's name and which parents belong to it. Another parent joins with an invitation code (usable once, valid for 7 days).
  • The children you add: a name or nickname, a colour and, if you like, a birthday. Children have no account of their own. A nickname is enough.

All parents in a family see and change the same data.

The clock

Your routines: the tasks, their duration, colours and pictures.

The calendar

  • The people who matter to your child (e.g. parents, grandparents, the babysitter): a name, other spellings of that name, if you like a birthday and a photo, and whether they pick your child up.
  • The settings of each calendar: school hours, who picks up when, after-school care, the size of your frame and the day pictures.
  • The pictures and photos you upload.

Only add photos of other people if they agree. For every photo, we remove the hidden data it contains (such as where it was taken). The calendar sheets (PDF) are made when you ask for them; we don't keep them.

Google Calendar (only if you connect the calendar)

The calendar takes its activities from Google Calendar. For that we ask your permission separately, and you choose how:

  • New calendar: Cork-e creates a separate calendar for your child in your Google account. We then only have access to calendars Cork-e created itself (calendar.app.created).
  • Existing calendar: you pick a calendar you already use. We then get access to events (calendar.events) and to the list of your calendars, so you can choose one (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). We only read and change events in the calendar you pick.

We use this access only to make the calendar sheets and to change events when you do so in Cork-e. We keep no copy of your events: we read them from Google each time you open the calendar. To keep the connection, we store a key from Google, encrypted.

Cork-e's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

On your device

The clock keeps a working copy of your routines in your browser, so it also works without internet, plus a few settings (e.g. which routine is open). That copy is removed when you sign out, and also by itself when your device hasn't been able to reach us for 30 days.

On the TV (Chromecast)

If you show the clock on a Chromecast, Chrome loads software from Google for it (Google Cast), and Google receives your IP address. The clock goes to the TV over your own network; the TV fetches the clock page from us. We store nothing in the process.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no profiles. Our server keeps no log of visitors' IP addresses. We don't sell or share your data.

Why are we allowed to do this?

  • Your account, your family, the clock and the calendar: to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • The Google Calendar connection: based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Backups and security: our legitimate interest in not losing your data (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Want your account erased? Email info@cork-e.eu. We then erase your data and withdraw the access to Google Calendar. If you are the last parent in your family, we also erase all of your family's data, pictures and photos. Your events in Google Calendar stay; they are yours.

Your rights

You may access your data, have it corrected or erased, receive a copy, have its processing restricted, and object. Email info@cork-e.eu; we reply within a month. You can also withdraw the Google access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

This website

The website cork-e.eu uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs technical data such as your IP address for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We tell you personally about important changes.
