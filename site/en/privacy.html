Cork-e privacy policy

Your account and your family · last updated 25 October 2026

This policy covers what all Cork-e tools share: your account and your family. What each tool stores on top of that is on its own page:

  • Cork-e Klok: the routine clock
  • Cork-e Kalender: calendar sheets from Google Calendar

In short

  • We only store what the tools need to work.
  • Everything is on our own server in Germany.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics. We count how often each family uses the tools, on our own server, and you can have that stopped. We don't sell or share anything.
  • One email to info@cork-e.eu is enough to have everything erased.

Who is responsible?

Cork-e is a project by Jessa Bekker, Belgium. Cork-e is still in development and is being tested with a small group of families. For any privacy question: info@cork-e.eu.

Your account

You sign in with your Google account. Google then gives us your name, your email address and a fixed identifier of your Google account. We use these to recognise you. We get no access to your password or your email.

After you sign in, we set one cookie that keeps you signed in (at most 180 days). While you sign in, we also use a 10-minute cookie to check that Google's answer belongs to your request. We use no other cookies.

Your family

  • Your family's name and which parents belong to it. Another parent joins with an invitation code (usable once, valid for 7 days).
  • The children you add: a name or nickname, a colour and, if you like, a birthday. Children have no account of their own. A nickname is enough.

All parents in a family see and change the same data, including the tools' data.

Counting use

To see whether Cork-e works as intended, we count per family and per day how often certain things happen: for example that the calendar was opened, that a child, a routine, a calendar or a person was added, or that calendar sheets were printed. We only keep the day and the number: not who did it, not which child, not what it contained, no time of day and no IP address. We set no cookie for this and store nothing on your device.

After 12 months, or when you erase your account, we merge these counts into weekly totals for all families together, no longer linked to your family.

If you don't want this, turn off Gebruik tellen (counting use): in the calendar, click your name at the top right. It applies to your whole family. Or email info@cork-e.eu. We then stop counting for your family and erase what we had counted.

Requesting a tester account

While Cork-e is in its test phase, you can only sign in with a Google account that is on our list of testers. When you request a tester account, we send your email address, the tool you want to use and your message (if you write one) as an email to info@cork-e.eu. Our server keeps none of it. We only use your address to put you on the list and to email you about it. To prevent abuse, the server remembers for one hour how often a request came from your IP address, in memory only.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, no profiles. Our server keeps no log of visitors' IP addresses. We don't sell or share your data.

Why are we allowed to do this?

  • Your account and your family: to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Requesting a tester account: you ask for it yourself, to be able to use the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Backups and security: our legitimate interest in not losing your data (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Counting use: our legitimate interest in keeping the tools working and improving them (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can always object; we then stop.

Your rights

You may access your data, have it corrected or erased, receive a copy, have its processing restricted, and object. Email info@cork-e.eu; we reply within a month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

This website

The website cork-e.eu uses no cookies and no analytics, and runs on the same server as the tools.

Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We tell you personally about important changes.
