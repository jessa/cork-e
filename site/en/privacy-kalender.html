Cork-e Kalender privacy policy

Calendar sheets from Google Calendar · last updated 21 October 2026

This page adds to the Cork-e privacy policy, about your account and your family. That page also says who is responsible, what your rights are and how to have everything erased.

In short

  • Your events stay in Google Calendar: we keep no copy of them.
  • We ask for Google Calendar access separately, only when you connect the calendar.
  • We keep photos of people only to show them on the calendar sheets.
  • Your pictures and photos are kept in one place, with your account; no web address shows them.

What the calendar stores

  • The people who matter to your child (e.g. parents, grandparents, the babysitter): a name, other spellings of that name, if you like a birthday (the year may be left out) with where it is shown, a photo, and whether they pick your child up.
  • The settings of each calendar: school hours, who picks up when, after-school care, the size of your frame and the day pictures.
  • The pictures and photos you upload. Together they are your family's pictures, for all your calendars; a person's photo is a picture from that set. Separate from it: pictures for a single activity and each calendar's day pictures.

Only add photos of other people if they agree. For every photo, we remove the hidden data it contains (such as where it was taken). The calendar sheets (PDF) are made when you ask for them; we don't keep them.

We keep your pictures and photos in one place: on our server, with your account. The part of Cork-e that draws the calendar sheets fetches them there with your sign-in and only keeps them briefly in its memory. No web address shows them; your browser only gets small previews inside the page and the calendar sheets themselves.

When you remove someone from Mensen (People), or replace or remove someone's photo, we ask whether that photo may go from your family's pictures too. The default answer is yes. If someone else still uses the same photo, it stays (and we say so).

Google Calendar

The calendar takes its activities from Google Calendar. For that we ask your permission separately, and you choose how:

  • New calendar: Cork-e creates a separate calendar for your child in your Google account. We then only have access to calendars Cork-e created itself (calendar.app.created).
  • Existing calendar: you pick a calendar you already use. We then get access to events (calendar.events) and to the list of your calendars, so you can choose one (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). We only read and change events in the calendar you pick.

We use this access only to make the calendar sheets and to change events when you do so in Cork-e. We keep no copy of your events: we read them from Google each time you open the calendar. To keep the connection, we store a key from Google, encrypted. While you connect, we use a 10-minute cookie to check that Google's answer belongs to your request.

You can withdraw this access yourself at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Cork-e's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Why are we allowed to do this?

  • The calendar, the people and the pictures: to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • The Google Calendar connection: based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
